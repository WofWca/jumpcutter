🎯 How to Test

1
Look for the Jump Cutter control button in the top-right corner of this page
2
Click the button to expand the control panel
3
Adjust the settings:
  • Sounded Speed: Speed during speech (try 1.0x)
  • Silence Speed: Speed during silence (try 2.0x or higher)
  • Volume Threshold: What counts as silence (5-10%)
4
Play the video and observe how silent parts are automatically sped up
